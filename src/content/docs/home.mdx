mdSummary: |
  Testsigma documentation covers two products.

  ## Testsigma

  A low-code test automation platform. You describe a test in plain English,
  and Testsigma runs it against web, mobile, desktop, Salesforce, and API
  applications without you writing test code. Atto, the AI layer, generates
  test cases from a Jira story, a design, or a prompt; auto-healing repairs
  locators that move; and results feed back into your CI pipeline.

  - New to Testsigma: read [Overview](/get-started/overview/), then
    [Concepts](/get-started/concepts/) and
    [Projects and applications](/get-started/projects-and-applications/).
  - Writing tests: [Create test cases](/create-and-manage/create-test-cases/)
    covers the four routes to the same steps: Atto, Copilot, the recorder, and NLPs.
  - Running tests: group cases into a [test suite](/run-tests/test-suite/),
    attach it to a [test plan](/run-tests/test-plan/), then read the
    [run result](/run-tests/results/).
  - Everything else: [Atto AI](/atto/ai-agents/),
    [dashboards](/analytics/dashboards/), and
    [integrations](/integrations/).
  - Reference: [REST API](/api/overview/),
    [Changelog](/changelog/), [installation](/get-started/installation/),
    [step types](/create-and-manage/step-types/),
    [addons](/addons/), and the [FAQ](/create-and-manage/faq/).

  ## Arcus

  A test management product. You store test cases, organize them into runs and
  plans, and track quality across releases. QI Home, the AI workspace inside
  Arcus, reads your requirements, generates test cases from them, and automates
  the ones you accept.

  - New to Arcus: read [Overview](/arcus/get-started/overview/).
  - Generating cases: [QI Home](/arcus/qi-home/overview/).
  - Writing and organizing: [Test cases](/arcus/create-and-manage/test-cases/).
  - Running and reporting: [Test runs](/arcus/run-tests/test-runs/).
  - Reference: [API reference](/arcus/api-reference/overview/) and
    [Changelog](/arcus/changelog/).
