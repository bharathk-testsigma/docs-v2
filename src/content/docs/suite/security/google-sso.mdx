Google single sign-on replaces passwords with your Google Workspace identity. One admin toggle enables it for the account. From the next sign-in, everyone on the team can log in with Google.

<Prerequisites>

- You are logged in to Google Workspace — the toggle stays disabled otherwise.

</Prerequisites>

## Enable it

<Steps>

1. **Open security settings** — From your profile, click **Switch Account**, then **Settings** under **Others**, then **Security (SSO)** under **Organization Settings**.

2. **Toggle Google** — Enable the **Google** widget.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
Only one SSO can be active per account. Enabling SAML (Okta, Azure) later means turning Google off first.
</Aside>
