Stuck on something? This is the home for troubleshooting guides, FAQs, and
how-tos across Testsigma Suite. Pick the area that matches your problem in the
left sidebar; it lists every page in each category.

## Browse by area

- **Agent & Setup**: agent registration, ports, proxies, on-prem and Docker setup, restricted-access issues.
- **Web Testing**: browser sessions, UI identifiers, page-load problems, and web-app FAQs.
- **Mobile Testing**: Android/iOS device issues, recorder failures, emulators/simulators, and mobile FAQs.
- **Steps & Elements**: element identification, dropdowns, checkboxes, file uploads, and step-level fixes.
- **Copilot & Terminal**: Copilot FAQs and Testsigma Terminal troubleshooting.
- **Execution & APIs**: API test failures, Salesforce testing, execution timing, downloads, and reporting issues.
- **Desktop & Windows**: desktop recorder and secured business application support.
- **General How-Tos**: cross-cutting FAQs and how-tos that aren't specific to one app type.

Can't find what you need? Use the search bar or the **Ask** assistant in the
header to search the full documentation.
