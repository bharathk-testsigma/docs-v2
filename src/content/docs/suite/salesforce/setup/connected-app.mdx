A connected app is Salesforce's door for external tools: it is what lets Suite authenticate, read your org's metadata, and drive tests. You create it once per org, then every Suite connection authenticates through its consumer key and secret.

<Prerequisites>

- You have a Salesforce organisation and Setup access.
- The org's edition has API access — some editions do not, and connections cannot be created in them.

</Prerequisites>

## Enable connected apps

<Steps>

1. **Open the settings** — In Salesforce **Setup**, search **Apps** in the quick finder and click **Settings** under **External Client Apps**.

2. **Allow creation** — Under **Connected Apps**, enable **Allow creation of connected apps** and confirm with **Enable**.

</Steps>

## Create the app

<Steps>

1. **Start it** — On the **External Client App Settings** page, click **New Connected App**.

2. **Name it** — Enter the **Connected App Name** and **Contact Email**; Salesforce fills the **API Name**. Everything else is optional.

3. **Enable OAuth** — In the **API** section, tick **Enable OAuth Settings**. Set the **Callback URL** to `https://salesforce-callback.testsigma.com/authorize/` and select the OAuth scopes — they are mandatory for metadata download.

4. **Save** — Click **Save**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
Wait 10–15 minutes after creating the app before connecting — Salesforce needs that long to propagate it. And a connected app holds a limited number of approvals, silently revoking the oldest past the limit, so create a separate app for each application connection.
</Aside>

## Fetch consumer details

Edit the connected app and click **Manage Consumer Details** (with no API-section checkboxes selected). The consumer key and secret it shows are what every [Suite connection](/suite/salesforce/setup/connections/) asks for.
