Zephyr Cloud integration links Zephyr test cases and test plans to their Testsigma counterparts. Run results export back to Zephyr as plans execute. Connecting takes a Zephyr access token plus your Jira account details.

<Prerequisites>

- A **Personal Access Token** from Zephyr Cloud, and your Jira **Account URL**, **Username**, and **API key**.

</Prerequisites>

## Connect

Go to **Settings → Integrations** and turn on **Zephyr Cloud**. Select the **Region of your Zephyr instance**. Enter the **Personal Access Token** and **Jira details**, then click **Save & Enable**.

## Link artifacts

Test case: copy its ID from Zephyr. Open the Testsigma test case, click **Manage Test Case**, enter the ID, and click **Link**. Test plan: the same flow from the test plan's own page.
