Trello integration pushes bugs from a run result onto your board. Cards arrive with annotations, the execution video, and step screenshots. Connecting takes the Trello API key together with a user token.

<Prerequisites>

- The **API Key** and **User Token** from your Trello account.

</Prerequisites>

## Connect

<Steps>

1. **Open integrations** — Go to **Settings → Integrations** and enable the **Trello** toggle.

2. **Enter the details** — **API Key** and **Token**.

3. **Save** — Click **Save & Enable**.

</Steps>

Then report from any **Test Case Result**: click **Report Bug**, choose **Trello**, fill the bug details (or **Link to Issue** for an existing card), and click **Create Ticket**. Only tools enabled on the integrations page appear in the chooser.
