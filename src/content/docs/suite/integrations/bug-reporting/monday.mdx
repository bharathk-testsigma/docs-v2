monday.com integration lets you file a bug in monday.com from a run result. The ticket carries the test's evidence: annotations, step screenshots, and the execution video. Setup needs two monday.com values.

<Prerequisites>

- Your organization's monday.com **Account URL** and a monday.com **API Key** (token).

</Prerequisites>

## Connect monday.com

<Steps>

1. **Enable the widget**: go to **Settings > Integrations** and turn on the toggle on the **monday.com** widget.

2. **Enter the details**: in the **Monday.com details** dialog, enter the **Account URL**, **API Key**, and **Confirm API Key**, then click **Save & Enable**.

</Steps>

## Report a bug

<Steps>

1. **Open the result**: go to **Run Results** and open the **Test Case Result** where you want to add a bug.

2. **Start the report**: click **Report Bug**.

3. **Pick monday.com**: in the **Report** overlay, click the **Monday.com** icon.

4. **File it**: enter the bug details in the form and click **Report Bug**.

</Steps>

![The Report panel, with Monday.com picked as the bug tracker](https://s3.amazonaws.com/website-static-docs.testsigma.com/new_images/projects/Updated_Doc_Images/minticon.png)

<Aside type="tip">
To attach the result to an existing monday.com issue instead, click **Link to Issue**, search for the issue, and click **Link Issue**.
</Aside>

When the bug is created, Testsigma automatically adds test result context to the monday.com issue, including the test execution video and step screenshots.
