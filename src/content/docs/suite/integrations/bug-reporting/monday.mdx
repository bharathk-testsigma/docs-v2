monday.com integration pushes annotated issues onto your board mid-session. One click carries the screenshots and context. Connecting takes the account URL and an API key; reporting happens from any test case result.

<Prerequisites>

- Your organisation's **monday.com account URL** and an **API token**.

</Prerequisites>

## Connect

<Steps>

1. **Open integrations** — Go to **Settings → Integrations** and enable the **monday.com** toggle.

2. **Enter the details** — **Account URL**, **API Key**, and its confirmation.

3. **Save** — Click **Save & Enable**.

</Steps>

Then report from any run: open the failing **Test Case Result**, click **Report Bug**, pick the **Monday.com** icon, review, and click **Report Bug**. **Link to Issue** attaches an existing ticket instead. Testsigma adds the execution video and step screenshots to the created issue automatically.
