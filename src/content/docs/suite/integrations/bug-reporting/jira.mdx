Jira integration pushes bugs from a run result straight into a Jira project. Screenshots, annotations, video, and step context travel with the ticket. Connecting takes your account URL, username, and an API token.

<Prerequisites>

- Your Jira **Account URL** (`https://<organization>.atlassian.net/`), your **username** (email), and a Jira **API token**.

</Prerequisites>

## Connect

<Steps>

1. **Open integrations** — Go to **Settings → Integrations** and enable the **Jira** toggle.

2. **Enter the details** — In the **Jira Details** dialog: **Account URL**, **Username**, **API Key**, and its confirmation.

3. **Save** — Click **Save & Enable**.

</Steps>

Then report from any run: open the failing **Test Case Result**, click **Report Bug**, pick the **Jira** icon, review, and click **Report Bug**. **Link to Issue** attaches an existing ticket instead. Testsigma adds the execution video and step screenshots to the created issue automatically.
