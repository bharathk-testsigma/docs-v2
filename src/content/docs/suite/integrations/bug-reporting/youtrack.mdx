YouTrack integration pushes bugs from a run result straight into a YouTrack project. Screenshots, annotations, and step context travel with the ticket. Connecting takes your account URL and a permanent API token.

<Prerequisites>

- Your organization's YouTrack **Account URL** and a YouTrack **API Key** (permanent token).

</Prerequisites>

## Create the API token

<Steps>

1. **Open your profile**: in YouTrack, go to your profile.

2. **Start a token**: go to **Account Security** and click **New Token**.

3. **Create it**: in **New Permanent Token**, enter the **Name** and **Scope**, then click **Create**.

4. **Copy it**: click **Copy Token**.

</Steps>

## Connect YouTrack

<Steps>

1. **Open integrations**: go to **Settings > Integrations**.

2. **Enable YouTrack**: turn on the toggle on the **Youtrack** widget.

3. **Enter the details**: in the **Youtrack Details** dialog, enter the **Organization URL** and **Access Key** (your token), then click **Save & Enable**.

</Steps>

## Report a bug

<Steps>

1. **Open the result**: go to **Run Results** and open the **Test Case Result** where you want to add a bug.

2. **Start the report**: click **Report Bug**.

3. **Pick YouTrack**: in the **Report** overlay, click the **Youtrack** icon.

4. **File it**: review the details and click **Report Bug**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
To attach the result to an existing YouTrack issue instead, click the **Link to Issue** tab, search for the issue, and click **Link To Ticket**.
</Aside>

When the bug is created, Testsigma automatically adds test result context to the YouTrack issue, including the test execution video and step screenshots.
