Bugzilla integration lets you file a bug in Bugzilla from a run result. The ticket carries the test's evidence: annotations, step screenshots, and the execution video. Setup needs two values from your Bugzilla account.

<Prerequisites>

- Your organization's Bugzilla **Account URL** and a Bugzilla **API Key**.

</Prerequisites>

## Connect Bugzilla

<Steps>

1. **Open integrations**: go to **Settings > Integrations**.

2. **Enable Bugzilla**: turn on the toggle on the **Bugzilla** widget.

3. **Enter the details**: in the **Bugzilla details** dialog, enter the **Account URL** and **API Key**, then click **Save & Enable**.

</Steps>

## Report a bug

<Steps>

1. **Open the result**: go to **Run Results** and open the **Test Case Result** where you want to add a bug.

2. **Start the report**: click **Report Bug**.

3. **Pick Bugzilla**: in the **Report** overlay, click the **Bugzilla** icon.

4. **File it**: enter the bug details in the form and click **Report Bug**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
To attach the result to an existing Bugzilla issue instead, click **Link to Issue**, search for the issue, and click **Link Issue**.
</Aside>

When the bug is created, Testsigma automatically adds test result context to the Bugzilla issue, including the test execution video and step screenshots.
