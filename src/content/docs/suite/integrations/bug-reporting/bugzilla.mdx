Bugzilla integration pushes annotated issues into a Bugzilla project mid-session. One click assigns them and attaches screenshots. Connecting takes the account URL and an API key; reporting happens from any test case result.

<Prerequisites>

- The **Account URL** — your organisation's Bugzilla URL — and an **API key** from your Bugzilla account.

</Prerequisites>

## Connect

<Steps>

1. **Open integrations** — Go to **Settings → Integrations** and enable the **Bugzilla** toggle.

2. **Enter the details** — In the **Bugzilla details** dialog: **Account URL** and **API Key**.

3. **Save** — Click **Save & Enable**.

</Steps>

Then report from any run: open the failing **Test Case Result**, click **Report Bug**, pick the **Bugzilla** icon, review, and click **Report Bug**. **Link to Issue** attaches an existing ticket instead. Testsigma adds the execution video and step screenshots to the created issue automatically.
