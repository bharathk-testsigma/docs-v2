Microsoft Teams integration posts run outcomes to a Teams channel. The setup mirrors Slack and Google Chat: one incoming webhook connector, one toggle in Suite, then per-plan notification settings choose what gets sent.

<Prerequisites>

- A **Microsoft Teams incoming webhook connector** URL for the target channel.

</Prerequisites>

## Connect

<Steps>

1. **Open integrations** — Go to **Settings → Integrations** and enable the **MS Teams** toggle.

2. **Enter the details** — **Name** and **Connector URL**, then **Save & Enable**.

</Steps>

## Turn it on per test plan

In **Create/Edit Test Plan → Test Plan Settings**, enable **Send Notification**. Pick the statuses under **Notify On** — pass, fail, abort, any combination. Then select **MS Teams** under **Also send messages to**.
