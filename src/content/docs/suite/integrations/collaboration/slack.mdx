Slack integration posts run outcomes to a channel as they happen. Pass, fail, and abort notifications each toggle separately per test plan. The connection is one incoming webhook URL taken from your own workspace.

<Prerequisites>

- A Slack **incoming webhook URL**.

</Prerequisites>

## Get the webhook

<Steps>

1. **Create an app** — In **Workspace → Tools & Settings → Manage Apps**, click **Build** to open the Slack API. Choose **Create an App → From Scratch**. Name it and pick the workspace.

2. **Activate webhooks** — Under **Incoming Webhooks**, enable **Activate Incoming Webhooks**. Click **Add New Webhook to Workspace**, pick the channel, and copy the URL.

</Steps>

## Connect

Go to **Settings → Integrations** and enable the **Slack** toggle. Enter the name and webhook URL, then click **Save & Enable**.

## Turn it on per test plan

In **Create/Edit Test Plan → Test Plan Settings**, enable **Send Notification**. Pick the statuses under **Notify On** — pass, fail, abort, any combination. Then select **Slack** under **Also send messages to**.
