Google Chat integration posts run outcomes into a Chat space. One webhook on the space, one toggle in Suite, and each test plan chooses which statuses notify. The webhook comes from the space's own settings.

<Prerequisites>

- A **Google Chat incoming webhook URL**.

</Prerequisites>

## Get the webhook

<Steps>

1. **Open the space** — In Google Chat, click the space name and select **Apps & Integrations**.

2. **Add the webhook** — Under **Integrations → Webhooks**, click **Add Webhook**. Name it — avatar optional — and click **Save**, then **Copy link**.

</Steps>

## Connect

Go to **Settings → Integrations** and enable the **Google Chat** toggle. Enter the **Name** and **Connector URL**, then click **Save & Enable**.

## Turn it on per test plan

In **Create/Edit Test Plan → Test Plan Settings**, enable **Send Notification**. Pick the statuses under **Notify On** — pass, fail, abort, any combination. Then select **Google Chat** under **Also send messages to**.
