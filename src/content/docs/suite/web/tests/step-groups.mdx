A step group is a sequence of steps you maintain in one place and reuse across test cases. Logging in is the usual example. Three test cases that each begin with a login share one group, not three copies.

<Prerequisites>

- You have a project with at least one test case.
- You know which steps repeat across those test cases.

</Prerequisites>

## Create a step group

<Steps>

1. **Open the list** — Go to **Create Tests → Step Groups**, then click **Create Step Group**.

2. **Name it** — Enter a **Name** for the group.

3. **Add the steps** — Click **Add new step** to write each step, or **Record** to capture them.

</Steps>

The group is available project-wide once its steps are saved. Insert it with the `/stepgroup` step type.

## Make a step group data-driven

A data-driven group runs its steps once per row of a test data profile. One login group then covers several user roles.

<Steps>

1. **Open the group settings** — Open the step group and click **Step Group Settings**.

2. **Link the data** — Select the **Test Data Profile** you want the group to read.

3. **Turn on data driving** — Enable the **Data Driven** toggle, then add the steps that use those values.

</Steps>

## Nest a step group

A step group can contain another step group, up to three levels deep. You build one by promoting steps you already have rather than creating an empty group.

<Steps>

1. **Select the steps** — In a test case or an open step group, tick the steps you want to group.

2. **Promote them** — Click **Create Step Group**.

3. **Name and swap** — Enter a **Name**, then click **Create and Replace**. The selected steps collapse into the new group in place.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
Three levels is the hard limit, in both test cases and step groups. A fourth level is refused rather than silently flattened.
</Aside>

## Edit a step group

Open the group from **Create Tests → Step Groups** and change the steps.

<Aside type="note">
An edit applies everywhere the group is used. A fix for one test case can break another. Check the affected cases before saving.
</Aside>

## Delete a step group

<Steps>

1. **Start the deletion** — Click the kebab menu beside the group, then click **Delete**.

2. **Read the affected list** — The **Deletion Warning** dialog lists every test case that uses the group. Deleting removes the group from all of them.

3. **Confirm** — Type `DELETE`, then click **I understand, delete this Step Group**.

</Steps>
