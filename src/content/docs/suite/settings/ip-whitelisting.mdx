Restricted access works in two directions. If your administrator blocks outbound traffic, Testsigma's domains need a network exception before you can open the application. If your application sits behind a firewall, Testsigma's cloud IP ranges need one too.

## Allow access to Testsigma

Ask your network administrator to whitelist these domains:

| Domain | What it unblocks |
| --- | --- |
| `*.testsigma.com` | The Testsigma application itself |
| `*.freshchat.com` | Testsigma Chat Support, for live chat with the support team |
| `*.freshdesk.com` | Testsigma Help Desk, for creating tickets and checking their status |

## Allow Testsigma to reach your application

Cloud test machines reach your application from Testsigma's own IP ranges. The **Testsigma IP** info screen under **Settings** publishes them for your account:

- **Testsigma Server IP**: the address of the Testsigma server, where executions on Testsigma's device cloud run.
- **Testsigma Lab IPs**: the ranges for Testsigma Labs, where the account's test assets and data are hosted.

Share the listed ranges with your network administrator or infosec team to allow them.

Allowlisting is one of three routes to a private application. [Testsigma Tunnel](/suite/get-started/installation/testsigma-tunnel/) opens a secure outbound connection instead. The [Testsigma Agent](/suite/get-started/installation/testsigma-agent/) runs tests on your own machines, so traffic never leaves your network.

## Troubleshooting

### You cannot open the Testsigma application

Network restrictions are blocking the domain. Request that your administrator whitelist `*.testsigma.com`.

### Chat support or the Help Desk does not load

Whitelist `*.freshchat.com` and `*.freshdesk.com` to reach the customer support portals.

### Tests pass on a local device but fail in Testsigma Lab

The cloud machines cannot reach an application behind your firewall. Allow the ranges from the **Testsigma IP** info screen, or route around the firewall with Testsigma Tunnel or the Testsigma Agent.
