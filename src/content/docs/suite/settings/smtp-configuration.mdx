SMTP configuration routes Testsigma's emails through your own mail server. Notifications then arrive from your domain rather than the default sender. The setup takes a host, port, credentials, and any extra parameters your server needs.

<Prerequisites>

- The SMTP server address, port, valid credentials, and any additional parameters.

</Prerequisites>

## Configure it

<Steps>

1. **Open the setting** — Go to **Settings → SMTP Configuration** and turn the toggle on.

2. **Choose the sender** — **Testsigma** keeps the default sender. **@own** takes your server's details. Enter the host such as `smtp.gmail.com`, the port such as `587`, and the username. The password is an app password generated by your mail provider.

3. **Save** — Click **Save**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
Turning the SMTP toggle off stops all email — it is the master switch, not just the custom-server switch.
</Aside>
