A phone number provisioned by Testsigma receives real SMS messages during a run. A test case enters the number in your app's login form. It then reads the one-time password out of the incoming message. No human with a phone is required.

## How it works

Testsigma allocates numbers to your account on request. Contact **support@testsigma.com** or use the in-app chat. In a step, the **% Phone Number** [test data type](/suite/web/test-data/test-data-types/) lists your allocated numbers. A `PhoneNumberFunctions` [data generator](/suite/web/test-data/data-generators/) then extracts the OTP from the SMS with a regex.

## When it applies

Use a phone number wherever the app sends an SMS the test must read: two-factor authentication, OTP login, and phone-based signup. A toggle on each number allows reading messages outside test executions. Runs behave the same either way.

## What it does not do

Phone numbers are not enabled by default; they are allocated per account by support. In some countries, provisioning phone numbers is restricted. There, install the Testsigma SMS Forwarder app instead. It forwards SMS from your own device to a [mailbox](/suite/settings/mail-boxes/) your tests read.
