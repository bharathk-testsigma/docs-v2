A mailbox is a Testsigma-hosted inbox attached to your account. Tests enter its address in a signup or login form, then read the email that arrives — an OTP, a verification link, a subject line — while the run is still going.

## How it works

The **& Mail Box** [test data type](/suite/web/test-data/test-data-types/) inserts the inbox address into a step. `MailBoxFunctions` [data generators](/suite/web/test-data/data-generators/) then read the incoming email: the OTP, a link, the subject, or the full content. Reads target the first matching email. `MailBoxAliasFunctions` generates a unique alias per run, so signup tests never hit "email already registered".

## When it applies

Use a mailbox wherever the app sends an email the test must read: email OTP for two-factor authentication, verification links in signup flows, and content checks — including email in languages other than English. A toggle also allows reading the mailbox outside test executions; runs behave the same either way.

## What it does not do

Mailboxes are not enabled by default; Testsigma allocates them per account — contact **support@testsigma.com** or use the in-app chat. When an app refuses to deliver to the hosted inbox, the Testsigma SMS Forwarder app is the fallback: it forwards messages from your own device into the mailbox.
