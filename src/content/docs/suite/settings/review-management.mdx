Review management puts an approval step between authoring and trusting. A test case or element goes to a reviewer, who approves it or sends it back for rework. Both work the same way, and both allow reviewing your own work.

<Prerequisites>

- A [project](/suite/get-started/projects-and-applications/) with test cases, and elements if you are reviewing those.
- [Users and roles](/suite/settings/users/) are set up, so there is someone to assign as reviewer.
- The relevant toggle is on under **Settings > Preferences**.

</Prerequisites>

## The two review modes

| Mode | Who reviews |
| --- | --- |
| Peer review | The author asks another team member to review, which spreads responsibility for automation quality across the team |
| Self-review | The author reviews their own work, handling every stage themselves |

Self-review is otherwise identical to peer review. The only difference is that the author assigns the review to themselves.

## Turn it on

<Steps>

1. **Open preferences** — Click **Settings** in the left navigation, then **Preferences**.

2. **Enable test case review** — Turn on the **Test Case Review Management** toggle.

3. **Enable self-review** — Turn on **Self-Review Management** if authors should be able to review their own work.

4. **Enable element review** — Turn on **Element Review Management** to review elements as well.

</Steps>

## Submit a test case for review

<Steps>

1. **Build the case** — Create a test case with its test steps.

2. **Submit it** — Click the **Submit for Review** icon in the top-right corner.

3. **Choose a reviewer** — Select a **Reviewer** from the dropdown, tick **Notify Assignee and Reviewer**, and add comments.

4. **Send it** — Click **Submit**.

</Steps>

The **Submitted for Review** section of the dashboard tracks what you have sent.

## Review a test case

<Steps>

1. **Find the request** — Open the link in the notification email, or the **Assigned for your Review** section on the dashboard.

2. **Read it** — Work through the test case.

3. **Give a verdict** — Click **Review Testcase** in the top-right corner.

4. **Record it** — In **Update Review Status**, tick **Notify Assignee and Reviewer**, add comments, and click **Approve** or **Rework**.

</Steps>

Rework sends it back to the author, and the cycle repeats until the case is approved.

## Submit an element for review

<Steps>

1. **Open the element** — Go to **Create Tests > Elements** and click the element.

2. **Edit it** — Click **Edit** in the **Element Details** overlay.

3. **Submit it** — Click **Submit for Review** in the **Update Element** overlay.

4. **Choose a reviewer** — Select a **Reviewer**, tick **Notify Assignee and Reviewer**, add comments, and click **Submit**.

</Steps>

## Review an element

<Steps>

1. **Find the request** — On the **Dashboard**, go to **Assigned for your review** and click **Elements**.

2. **Open it** — Click the element you are reviewing. The **Elements** page opens filtered to your assignments.

3. **Start the review** — Hover the element, click the kebab menu, and select **Edit**.

4. **Give a verdict** — Click **Review element**, then mark **Approve** or **Rework** in **Update review status**.

5. **Save it** — Back in **Update Element**, click **Update Element**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
An element review is not recorded until you click **Update Element** at the end. Marking the status and closing the overlay loses the verdict.
</Aside>
