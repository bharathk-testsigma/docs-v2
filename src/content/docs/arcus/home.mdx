mdSummary: |
  Testsigma Arcus is a test management product. You store test cases, organize
  them into runs and plans, and track quality across releases. QI Home, the AI
  workspace inside Arcus, reads your requirements, generates test cases from
  them, and automates the ones you accept.

  Where to start:

  - New to Arcus: read [Overview](/arcus/get-started/overview/) for what Arcus
    does, the objects you work with, and where to start.
  - Generating cases: [QI Home](/arcus/qi-home/overview/) covers the workspace
    that turns a requirement into test cases you review and accept.
  - Writing and organizing: [Test cases](/arcus/create-and-manage/test-cases/)
    covers cases, reusable step groups, and the data sets they read.
  - Running and reporting: [Test runs](/arcus/run-tests/test-runs/) covers
    executing a run, tracking it in a plan, and reporting the outcome.
  - Integrations: Jira and Linear for requirements, Jenkins and GitHub for
    pipelines, Figma and Claude for design and AI tooling.
