Coverage is the percentage of generated test cases you have accepted. Gaps are the cases still pending. Both update in real time as you review, so the **Coverage** tab is where you decide what to work through next.

## How coverage is calculated

**Coverage = Accepted ÷ (Accepted + Pending) × 100**

If QI Home generated 10 cases and you accepted 3, coverage is 30% and the other 7 are gaps. Rejected cases are excluded. Coverage shows at two levels: for the whole session in the header, and per module in the **Coverage** tab.

## View coverage and gaps

<Steps>

1. **Go to QI Home** — Select a sprint or Ad-Hoc session from the sidebar.

2. **Open the tab** — Click **Coverage**.

</Steps>

Test cases are grouped by module. Each module row shows its name, coverage percentage, test case count, and gap count.

## Review gaps by module

<Steps>

1. **Expand a module** — Click the module name. Each gap shows its title, story ID, test type, and **Pending** status.

2. **Decide** — Click a gap to open the detail view, then accept or reject it.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
  Gaps are not failed tests. They are generated cases you have not reviewed yet.
  Accepting one removes it from the list and raises your coverage.
</Aside>

## Coverage in the session header

The header shows coverage at a glance, next to the module count. It starts at 0% and climbs as you accept. **Pass Rate**, **Confidence**, and **Release Readiness** show **Awaiting execution** until runs happen. **Release Gate** shows **Not Ready** until its thresholds are met. All five are explained in [QI metrics](/arcus/qi-home/qi-metrics/).

To fill gaps with new test cases, open the [Playground](/arcus/qi-home/use-the-playground/) from the session header.
