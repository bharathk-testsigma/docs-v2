Generated test cases arrive in **Pending** status — they are not in your library yet. You review them, accept the accurate ones, and save them. The flow is identical for sprint and Ad-Hoc sessions. Anything left pending counts against your coverage as a gap.

## Before you begin

- You have at least one sprint or Ad-Hoc session with generated test cases.

## Browse test cases by scenario

<Steps>

1. **Go to QI Home** — Select a session from the sidebar. The **Tests** tab opens by default.

2. **Expand a scenario** — Click the arrow next to a scenario name.

</Steps>

The header shows the session title, case count, the **Accepted** / **Pending** / **Rejected** status bar, and the five [QI metrics](/arcus/qi-home/qi-metrics/). Each expanded scenario shows its **Impact** — the affected modules as tags — and its test cases with priority, name, module, type, and status.

## Review an individual test case

<Steps>

1. **Open the case** — Click a test case name. The detail view shows its tags, description, preconditions, and numbered steps with expected results.

2. **Decide** — Click **Save to Library** if it looks right, or **Reject** if not.

</Steps>

## Accept or reject in bulk

<Steps>

1. **Select cases** — Tick the checkbox next to each test case. A bottom action bar appears with **Select All**, **Reject**, and **Save to Library**.

2. **Act on them** — Click **Save to Library** to accept the selection, or **Reject** to dismiss it.

</Steps>

## Save to your library

<Steps>

1. **Open the dialog** — Click **Save to Library** on a case or on the bulk action bar.

2. **Pick a folder** — Browse the folder hierarchy and select one.

3. **Save** — Click **Save**. The cases move from **Pending** to **Accepted**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
  QI Home suggests a folder based on your project's feature taxonomy. Accept it or
  pick another.
</Aside>

Rejected cases are dismissed and do not count toward coverage. Pending cases count against your coverage until you decide. The percentage updates in real time as you accept — see [Coverage and gaps](/arcus/qi-home/coverage-and-gaps/).
