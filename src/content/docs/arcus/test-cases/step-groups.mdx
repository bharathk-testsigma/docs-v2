A step group is a reusable sequence of steps shared across test cases. Instead of repeating the same steps in every case, you link the group — and editing it once updates every linked case.

## Create a step group

<Steps>

1. **Start one** — Go to **Step Groups** and click **Create Step Group**.

2. **Fill it** — Enter a **Title** and optional **Description**, then add the test steps. Attachments up to 25 MB are supported.

3. **Save** — Click **Create Step Group**.

</Steps>

## Create a step group from a test case

Existing steps can become a group without retyping them.

<Steps>

1. **Select the steps** — On the **Test Case Details** page, click **Create Step Group** and tick the steps to include.

2. **Save** — Click **Create Step Group**. The creation page opens in a new tab — adjust the steps and save.

</Steps>

## Add a step group to a test case

<Steps>

1. **Link it** — On the **Test Case Details** page, click **Add Step Group**, select the group, and click **+**.

</Steps>

## Edit a step group

<Steps>

1. **Open it** — In **Step Groups**, pick **Edit** from the group's kebab menu.

2. **Change it** — Update the name, description, attachments, or steps. Changes auto-save.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
  Edits apply to every test case the group is linked to.
</Aside>

## Delete a step group

<Steps>

1. **Delete** — Pick **Delete** from the group's kebab menu, tick the confirmation checkbox, and click **Delete**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
  Deleting unlinks the group from every linked test case, permanently.
</Aside>
