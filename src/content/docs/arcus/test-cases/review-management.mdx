Review Management adds an approval workflow to test cases. Authors submit a case to a named reviewer. The reviewer marks it ready, or returns it for rework with comments. Every action lands in the case's **Activity** section.

## Before you begin

- The team members who will review are [invited to Arcus](/arcus/settings/invite-users/).

## Enable Review Management

<Steps>

1. **Open project settings** — Go to **Project → Project Settings**.

2. **Turn it on** — Enable the **Review Management** toggle.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
  This is a project-level setting — enable it separately in each project that
  needs it.
</Aside>

## Submit test cases for review

<Steps>

1. **Open the case** — In **Test Cases**, open the case and click **Submit for Review**.

2. **Pick the reviewer** — Select a **Reviewer** in the dialog and click **Submit for Review**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
  To submit several at once, select them in the list, press **Command + K**, and
  pick **Submit for Review**.
</Aside>

## Review test cases

<Steps>

1. **Find the queue** — On the **Dashboard**, go to **Test Case Review Status** and click a submitted case.

2. **Decide** — Click **Mark as Ready** to approve. Or click **Mark as Rework** and submit your comments.

</Steps>
