SMTP configuration makes Arcus send email notifications from your organization's own authenticated address instead of the default Testsigma sender. Your domain on the envelope means better branding, deliverability, and security.

## Before you begin

- You have your provider's SMTP server address, port, username and password, and any extra parameters.

## Configure SMTP

<Steps>

1. **Open the section** — Go to **Settings → SMTP Configuration** and turn on the **SMTP Configuration** toggle.

2. **Enter your server** — Select **@own** and fill in the **Host**, **Port**, **Username**, and **Password**.

3. **Save** — Click **Save**. Every email notification now goes out from your address.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
  Selecting **Testsigma** instead keeps the default Testsigma sender.
</Aside>
