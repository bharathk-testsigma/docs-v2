Collaboration starts with inviting your team. Each invite carries a role. The role decides what the user can do in the workspace, from full control down to view-only. Invites go out by email, in batches if you like.

## Before you begin

- You have a [project](/arcus/get-started/projects/) and permission to invite team members.

## Invite users

<Steps>

1. **Open the Users section** — Click **Invite Users** in the left nav, then **Invite Users** again on the page.

2. **Address the invite** — In the **Invite Team** dialog, enter one or more email addresses, separated by commas.

3. **Pick a role and send** — Select **Admin** or **Read Only**, then click **Send Invite**. Each invitee gets an email link.

</Steps>

The two roles:

| Role          | Access                                                            |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Admin**     | Full workspace access, including create, read, update, and delete |
| **Read Only** | View access to test entities, no changes                          |
