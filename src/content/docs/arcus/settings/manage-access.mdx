Admins can grant the Testsigma Support team temporary access for diagnosing an issue. Access is scoped to troubleshooting and expires on its own. You can also revoke it whenever you choose, from the same page that granted it.

## Grant or revoke access

<Steps>

1. **Open the section** — Go to **Settings → Manage Access** and click **Allow Access**.

2. **Scope it** — Select the **Access Type** and briefly explain the reason in **Describe the issue**.

3. **Grant** — Tick the consent checkbox and click **Submit**. The page shows the **Current Status** and **Access Expiry**.

4. **Revoke or change** — Click **Manage** on the same page at any time.

</Steps>

## Security and privacy

- Every support action is logged under **guest@testsigma.com**, for full traceability.
- Access is limited to what troubleshooting requires.
- You keep full control and can revoke at any moment.

Access logs live under **Activity** in the utility panel and are kept for 3 years.
