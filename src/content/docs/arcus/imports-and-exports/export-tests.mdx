Arcus exports test cases and step groups as one Excel file, with all their associated information. Use it for backups or migration. It also suits reviewing test data outside the platform, offline or in a spreadsheet.

## Export test cases

<Steps>

1. **Open the export widget** — Click **Import Cases** on the **Dashboard**, or open **Project Settings** directly.

2. **Start the export** — In the **Export Test Cases & Step Groups** widget, click **Export**. Confirm with **Start Export**.

3. **Download** — Click the **Download** icon on the most recent entry. The Excel file lands on your machine.

</Steps>
