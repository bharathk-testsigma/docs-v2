Connecting Figma to Arcus by Testsigma lets you generate test cases from your designs, turning UI elements into test steps.

<Prerequisites>
- An active project in Arcus by Testsigma.
- Your Figma **Team ID** and a Figma personal access token.
</Prerequisites>

## Get your Figma Team ID

<Steps>

1. Open Figma in a web browser.
2. In the left navigation, select your team from the dropdown.
3. Read the team ID from the address bar. In `https://www.figma.com/files/team/<TEAM_ID>/your-team-name`, the `<TEAM_ID>` segment is your Figma Team ID.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
If you can't reach the team page, ask your Figma admin for the Team ID.
</Aside>

## Generate a Figma personal access token

<Steps>

1. Open Figma in a web browser.
2. Click your profile icon, then select **Settings**.
3. Go to **Security > Personal access tokens**.
4. Click **Generate new token**.
5. In the **Generate new token** dialog, enter a **Token name**.
6. Select an expiration period from the **Expiration** dropdown.
7. Click **Generate token**.
8. Click **Copy**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
Store the token now. Figma doesn't show it again.
</Aside>

## Connect Figma

<Steps>

1. In the left navigation, go to **Settings > Integrations**.
2. Click **Setup Integration** next to Figma.
3. On the **Integrate Figma** prompt, enter the **Team ID** and **API Key**.
4. Click **Connect**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
The **API Key** field takes the personal access token you generated.
</Aside>
