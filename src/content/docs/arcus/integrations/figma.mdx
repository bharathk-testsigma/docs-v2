The Figma integration lets QI Home read your designs and [generate test cases](/arcus/qi-home/generate/attach-context-sources/) from UI frames. It needs two things from Figma: your Team ID and a personal access token. Both take a minute to collect.

## Before you begin

- You have a [project](/arcus/get-started/projects/) and access to your Figma team.

## Collect the Figma credentials

<Steps>

1. **Find the Team ID** — In Figma, select your team in the left nav. The URL reads `figma.com/files/team/{TEAM_ID}/your-team-name` — the number is your Team ID.

2. **Generate a token** — From your profile icon, open **Settings → Security → Personal access tokens**. Click **Generate new token**, name it, pick an **Expiration**, and click **Generate token**.

3. **Copy the token** — Click **Copy**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
  Store the token securely — Figma never shows it again. No access to the team
  page? Ask your Admin for the Team ID.
</Aside>

## Connect the integration

<Steps>

1. **Open integrations** — On the **Dashboard**, click **Integrations**, then **Setup Integration** next to the **Figma** icon.

2. **Connect** — Enter the **Team ID** and **API Key**, then click **Connect**.

</Steps>
