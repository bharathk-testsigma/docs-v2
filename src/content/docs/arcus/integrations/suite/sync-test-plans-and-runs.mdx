Linked test plans let you execute in Suite without leaving Arcus. A trigger from Arcus runs the Suite plan. Each machine's execution comes back as its own test run. Logs, screenshots, and result links ride along.

## Before you begin

- The [Suite integration](/arcus/integrations/suite/connect-suite/) is enabled, and test plans exist on both sides.

## Link from Arcus

<Steps>

1. **Open the plan** — Open the plan in **Test Plans**. Click **Link a Test Plan** under **Testsigma Automated Test Plan**.

2. **Pick the Suite plan** — Select the Suite **Project**, **Application**, **Version**, and **Test Plan**, then click **Link**.

</Steps>

On the Suite side, the link shows in the **Test Plan Details** overlay, under **Test Management Tool**.

## Link from Suite

<Steps>

1. **Open the plan** — In Suite's **Test Plans**, open the plan. Click **Test Plan Details** in the utility panel.

2. **Pick the Arcus plan** — Click **+ Link New Plans** and select **Testsigma Test Management**. Pick the project and plan, then click **Link Test Plan**.

</Steps>

In Arcus, the link shows under **Testsigma Automated Test Plan** when editing the plan.

## Execute a linked plan

<Steps>

1. **Trigger it** — On the plan's details page, click the **Play** icon next to the linked plan.

2. **Wait for the runs** — When Suite finishes, Arcus creates one test run per machine. Each holds that machine's executed cases.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
  Every trigger creates a fresh set of runs, one per machine.
</Aside>

## View automated reports

<Steps>

1. **Open the run** — In **Test Plans**, open the plan and then the test run. The right panel summarizes the execution.

2. **Drill in** — Click a test case to open its overlay, then scroll to **Activity**. The entry links to the Suite results page for the automated execution.

</Steps>
