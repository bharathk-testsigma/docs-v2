Linking makes one test case the shared truth of both products. A manual case in Arcus can push to Suite for automation. An automated case in Suite can link back as a manual case with steps. Either direction removes duplicate upkeep.

## Before you begin

- The [Suite integration](/arcus/integrations/suite/connect-suite/) is enabled, and test cases exist on both sides.

## Link from Arcus

<Steps>

1. **Open the case** — In **Test Cases**, open the case and click **Link a Test Case** in the right panel.

2. **Pick the automated case** — In the **Link Testsigma Automated Test Case** dialog, select the Suite **Project**, **Application**, and **Version**, tick the cases, and click **Link**.

</Steps>

The link shows under **Testsigma Automated Test Case** in the right panel. On the Suite side it appears under **Test Management Tool** in **Manage Test Case**.

## Link from Suite

<Steps>

1. **Open the case** — In Suite, go to **Create Tests → Test Cases**, open the case, and select **Manage Test Case** from the utility panel.

2. **Pick the manual case** — Click **+ Link New Tests**. Select **Testsigma Test Management** as the tool, pick the project and the case, and click **Link Test Case**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
  Turn on the **Link Multiple** toggle to link several cases in one pass.
</Aside>

## Sync generated cases from Atto's Home

Cases generated in Suite's Atto's Home can land in Arcus at the moment you accept them.

<Steps>

1. **Generate the steps** — In a session, select a test case and click **Generate Automated Steps** on the **Manual Steps** tab. The NLP steps appear under **Automated Steps**.

2. **Accept into Suite** — Click **Accept**, choose the folder and scenario, and click **Confirm**.

3. **Sync to Arcus** — Open the dropdown next to **Accept** and select **Accept & Sync with Testsigma TMS**. Pick the Arcus project and folder, then click **Continue**.

</Steps>

Arcus creates the manual case and links it to the Suite case automatically.

## Automate a manual test case

The reverse trip: an Arcus manual case becomes a Suite automation session.

<Steps>

1. **Start from Arcus** — Open the case and click **Automate with AI** on the details overlay. {/* UNVERIFIED: button label after QI Home removal (was "Automate with QI Home's AI") */}

2. **Aim at Suite** — Select the Suite **Project**, **Application**, and **Version**, then click **Continue**. Suite opens Atto's Home with the case loaded.

3. **Automate** — Click **Generate Automated Steps**, then **Agentic Learning** to learn the live application. Or skip straight to **Agentic Learning** from the **Automated Steps** tab, picking the **Environment** from its dropdown.

</Steps>
