The Suite integration links automated tests in Suite with their manual counterparts in Arcus. Execution results, logs, and screenshots sync both ways in real time, so coverage and quality stay visible across manual and automated testing without duplicate effort.

## Before you begin

- Both Arcus and Suite are on an Enterprise plan.

## Enable the integration

Either side can turn it on.

<Steps>

1. **From Arcus** — Go to **Settings → Integrations** and turn on the toggle next to **Testsigma**.

2. **Or from Suite** — Go to **Settings → Integrations** and turn on the toggle next to **Test Management by Testsigma**.

</Steps>

## Manage users across both products

With the integration on, one invite can grant access to both products. Roles are assigned per project in either flow.

<Steps>

1. **From Arcus** — Click **Invite Users**, then **Invite Users** again. In the dialog, enter the email, select **Add User in Testsigma**, pick the projects and roles, and click **Send Invite**.

2. **Or from Suite** — Go to **Settings → Users** and click **Add New User**. Select **Add User in Test Management by Testsigma**, pick the projects and roles, and click **Send Invite**.

</Steps>

Each invitee gets an email link to join the workspace.
