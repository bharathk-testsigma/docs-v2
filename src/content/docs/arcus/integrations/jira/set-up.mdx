The Jira integration works in two layers. The basic connection links defects in Arcus to Jira issues. The **Test Management by Testsigma** Jira app goes further: it puts test cases and runs inside Jira itself.

## Before you begin

- You have an active Jira account with the necessary permissions.

## Connect the integration

<Steps>

1. **Start the setup** — Go to **Settings → Integrations** and click **Setup Integration** next to the Jira icon. The Atlassian login opens in a new tab.

2. **Authorize** — Log in, select the app on the **Requesting Access** page from **Use app on**, and click **Accept**.

</Steps>

## Link defects to Jira

<Steps>

1. **Open the case** — In **Test Runs**, open a run and click the test case. The **Quick View** overlay opens.

2. **Add the defect** — Click **Add a Defect**. QI Home pre-fills the **Project**, **Issue Type**, **Status**, and **Description** — edit as needed.

3. **Pick the target** — On the **Link to Existing Issue** tab, choose the Jira issues. Or on **Create a New Issue**, fill in the issue fields and a **Summary**.

4. **Save** — Click **+ Add Defect**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">One defect can link to multiple existing Jira issues.</Aside>

## Install the Jira app

The app extends Jira with native test management — create, run, and track tests without switching to Arcus.

<Steps>

1. **Install it** — Search the [Atlassian Marketplace](https://marketplace.atlassian.com/) for **Test Management by Testsigma** and install it. Confirm under **Apps → Manage apps**.

2. **Open the app** — Click **Test Management by Testsigma** under **Apps** in the left panel. The authorization page opens.

3. **Authorize** — Click **Authorize to Jira Marketplace App**, then **Continue**, and sign in with your Testsigma credentials.

4. **Finish** — Review the account and click **Authorize Jira Integration**.

</Steps>
