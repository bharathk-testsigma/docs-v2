The Confluence integration lets QI Home read your pages — PRDs, specifications, release notes — and [generate test cases](/arcus/qi-home/generate/attach-context-sources/) from them. Connecting takes one authorization. The content stays in Confluence. Arcus reads it when you attach pages to a session.

## Before you begin

- You have an active Confluence account with the necessary permissions.

## Connect the integration

<Steps>

1. **Start the setup** — Go to **Settings → Integrations** and click **Setup Integration** next to the **Confluence** icon. The Atlassian login opens in a new tab.

2. **Authorize** — Log in and click **Accept** on the **Requesting Access** page.

</Steps>
