Reports turn selected test cases, test runs, or test plans into visual summaries of execution status, progress, and defects. You choose the artifacts; the report charts only those. Reports live on the **Reports** page, one tab per artifact type.

## Create a report

<Steps>

1. **Start one** — Go to **Reports** and click **Create Report**.

2. **Pick the data** — In **Select Data for Visualization**, choose the artifact type, attach the artifacts, and click **Attach Test Runs**.

3. **Name it** — Enter a **Name** and optional **Description**, then click **Create a Report**.

</Steps>

## Read a report

Open a report from its artifact tab — **Test Cases**, **Test Runs**, or **Test Plans**. Its sections chart the attached artifacts:

| Section                        | What it shows                                                       |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Test Execution Progress**    | Line chart of completed vs. remaining tests over time               |
| **Test Execution Status**      | Distribution of statuses, as a breakdown and a percentage bar chart |
| **Defect Report (Cumulative)** | Cumulative defects vs. newly raised or linked ones                  |
| **Defect Status**              | Donut chart of current defect states                                |
| **Comparative Analysis**       | Execution statuses grouped by assignee                              |

## Edit a report

<Steps>

1. **Open the editor** — Pick **Edit** from the report's kebab menu.

2. **Adjust** — Update the name or description, and click **Preview Selected Runs** to review the selection.

3. **Re-select** — Click **Update Selection**, change the runs, click **Update Test Runs**, then **Update Report**.

</Steps>

## Export a report

<Steps>

1. **Open the report** — Pick it from its artifact tab and click **Export**.

2. **Download** — Name the file in **Export Report as PDF** and click **Download PDF**.

</Steps>

## Delete a report

<Steps>

1. **Delete** — Pick **Delete** from the report's kebab menu and confirm with **Delete Report**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
  Editing, exporting, and deleting work the same for reports built on test
  cases, runs, or plans.
</Aside>
