The Dashboard shows your test activity in real time — case counts, execution status, automation coverage, run trends, defects, and review queues — without opening a report. Every chart respects the two filters at the top.

## Filters

**Date Range** limits the charts to a period: last 1 day, 7 days, 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, or all time. **Assigned To** narrows the data to one person, for tracking individual activity or workload.

<Aside type="tip">
  Combine both — **Last 1 month** plus one assignee — for targeted insights.
</Aside>

## Sections

Each section of the Dashboard answers one question:

| Section                           | Chart          | What it shows                                                                           |
| --------------------------------- | -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Summary metrics**               | Header numbers | Automation Coverage, Automated, Manual, and Total test case counts                      |
| **Active Runs**                   | Donut          | Status of active runs: Passed, Failed, Blocked, In Progress, Retest, Skipped, Untested  |
| **Closed Test Runs**              | Line           | Runs closed per date, for cycle completion patterns                                     |
| **Results from Closed Test Runs** | Stacked bar    | Case results from closed runs, grouped by status                                        |
| **Types of Test Cases**           | Donut          | Distribution across Functional, Integration, Non-Functional, Unit Test, User Experience |
| **Defects Logged**                | Bar            | Defects logged over the selected period                                                 |

**Active Runs**, **Closed Test Runs**, and **Types of Test Cases** each link through to their full list view.

## Test case review status

Two tables track the [review workflow](/arcus/test-cases/review-management/). **Awaiting My Review** lists cases assigned to you, with assignee and submitted time. **Submitted for Review** lists your own submissions and their reviewers.

## Export

Click **Export** on the Dashboard with your filters applied, name the file, and click **Download PDF**.
