Arcus runs in your browser, so there is nothing to install. All you need is a Testsigma account and a few minutes. This page takes you from sign-in to creating your first project.

## Before you begin

- You have an active Testsigma account. If not, create one on the [Testsigma signup page](https://tms.testsigma.com/signup).

<Aside type="note">
  Already using Suite? Contact **support@testsigma.com** to connect your
  existing account.
</Aside>

## Open Arcus

<Steps>

1. **Sign in** — Go to the [Arcus login page](https://tms.testsigma.com/login) and enter your credentials.

2. **Switch to Arcus** — In your **User Profile Settings**, select **TMS**. Arcus opens on the **Dashboard**.

3. **Create a project** — Click the **Candy Box** icon, then **+ New Project**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
  Your first login lands straight on project creation — skip step 3.
</Aside>
