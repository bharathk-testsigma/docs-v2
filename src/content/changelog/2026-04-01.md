## New features

**AI Field Mapping for CSV Imports** — Importing a CSV no longer means mapping every column by hand. Testsigma reads the headers and a sample of the data, maps them to the right fields, and lets you review and adjust before importing.

**Import Reports** — Every import reports how many test cases succeeded, failed, or were skipped, with the reason for each failure — a missing mandatory field, invalid data — and an explicit reason for anything skipped.
